Feel connected.

Launch films and story videos for the Murror 2.0 launch. The brief: make people feel the pull to reach out to someone they already love.

Role
Concept, script, direction, edit
Year

What I made.

9:16Story videos

Direction

“I set how the story looks and moves.”

Brand feeling

One feeling leads every piece: the pull to reach out to someone you already love.

Visual language

Far camera, a small figure in a wide space. Six shots, the last one always wide. One grade per film.

Motion and sound

Orbits, phones with the real app, quiet night skies. Soft piano that peaks into the ending.

Guardrails

A still storyboard is reviewed before anything is rendered. One look on every channel.

LEADERSHIP

“I get the whole team telling one story.”

Murror runs design sprints with the whole team. In the second sprint I led storytelling for the theme “Understand Murror in seconds”, so everyone tells the same story.
